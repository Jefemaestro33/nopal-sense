## Summary

Nopal-Sense is preparing for the June 12 Project Proposal Review. The public
scope has been clarified as a low-power mixed-signal soil/root-zone impedance
spectroscopy hub for agriculture. The first tapeout is framed around the
repeatable multi-frequency measurement instrument; root-zone stress or disease
interpretation, including possible fungal/oomycete-related patterns, is
explicitly deferred to off-chip software, validated datasets, and AI models.
